🌟 **Turning Screen Time into Learning Time — Introducing Scratch and Learn!** 🎮📚

I'm happy to share that I've developed and published **[Scratch and Learn](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rajeshtiwari.scratchandlearn)**, a fun educational game that helps kids learn ABCs, numbers, fruits, vegetables, and animals through interactive quizzes.

My goal is to make children's screen time more meaningful through fun and learning! 😊

---

### Why Scratch and Learn?

Young minds learn fastest when they actively interact with their environment. The classic physical scratch card provides a moment of surprise and delight—every stroke reveals something new.

**Scratch and Learn** translates this simple, captivating mechanic into a digital educational playground where kids uncover colorful illustrations and answer questions, refining their recognition and motor skills.

---

### ✨ What Makes It Special?

- 🎨 **Interactive Scratch Mechanics**: Kids actively scratch the screen to reveal hidden items rather than passively consuming video content.
- 🔤 **Comprehensive Learning Modules**: Covers alphabets (ABCs), counting numbers, fruits, vegetables, animals, and everyday objects.
- 🧠 **Interactive Quizzes & Puzzles**: Reinforces newly learned concepts with immediate, encouraging feedback.
- 🛡️ **Child-Safe & Family-Friendly**: Designed adhering strictly to Google Play Families Policies—zero invasive ads and zero deceptive prompts.
- 📴 **Offline Support**: Fully playable without an active internet connection, perfect for traveling or distraction-free learning.

---

### Tech & Architecture

- **React Native**: Built for smooth, fluid cross-platform mobile performance.
- **High-FPS Canvas Interactions**: Optimized scratch rendering to guarantee immediate touch response without battery drain.
- **Audio & Haptic Feedback**: Carefully timed cheerful sound effects and audio narration to keep young learners delighted.
